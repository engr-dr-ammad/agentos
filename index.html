Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe what you need, choose agents and connect them visually. Advanced users can still use webhooks, APIs and JSON schemas.

Which AI models can I use?

Bring your own: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Azure OpenAI, Mistral, Groq, DeepSeek, OpenRouter or a self-hosted model such as Ollama or vLLM. You can mix providers per agent.

Can an agent send an email on its own?

Not unless you allow it. Sending email, calling external APIs and similar actions require a human approval bound to the exact message, enforced on the server.

Where is my data?

Each organisation is isolated at the database level. API keys are encrypted and never shown to the browser or the AI model.

Is AgentOS compliant with the EU AI Act?

AgentOS includes a structured AI Act self-assessment and GDPR tools, but a checklist is not a legal determination. High-risk uses require qualified legal review.
